Featured Trip Packages

1. Family Scenic Float

Difficulty: Easy (Class I-II)

Perfect for families, beginners, and children. Enjoy smooth water, scenic river canyon views, gentle splashes, and a relaxed riverside lunch stop.

Duration: Half Day (3 Hours)

2. Main River Challenge

Difficulty: Moderate (Class III)

Our most popular trip! Experience exciting wave trains, technical paddles, and continuous action suitable for active first-timers and return paddlers.

Duration: Full Day (6 Hours)

3. Extreme Gorge Expedition

Difficulty: Advanced (Class IV-V)

Designed for adrenaline seekers. Fast-paced, steep drops, and intense rapids through narrow granite gorges. Requires strong swimming capability.

Duration: 2 Days (Overnight Camp)
