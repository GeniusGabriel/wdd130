Genius Expeditions Rafting Company

Our Mision

Our mission at Genius Expedition Rafting Company . is to provide thrill-seekers and families alike with safe, unforgettable white water adventures. We believe in connecting people with nature through exhilarating river expeditions guided by expert professionals.

History

Established in 2010, Genius Expeditions Rafting Company began with a single raft and a passion for sharing wild rivers. Over the past decade, we have safely guided thousands of adventure seekers down world-class rapids.

Our team of expert guides is committed to environmental conservation, water safety, and delivering memorable white water experiences for families, school groups, and thrill-seekers alike.
